Your metrics, in plain language

Spin efficiency
The share of your spin that actually moves the ball. 100% means all of it is working. Lower means more gyro spin, which is spin that does not move the ball.
Gyro degree
How much of the spin is bullet-spin. 0 is all movement spin. 90 is pure gyro: the ball spins like a bullet and barely moves.
Spin direction
The clock face your spin points to. 12 is straight backspin: the ball fights gravity and rides. 3 (righty) is pure sidespin: the ball runs arm-side. 6 is topspin: the ball dives.
Vertical break
Inches the spin lifts or drops the ball against gravity. Backspin rides, topspin dives.
Horizontal break
Inches the spin pushes the ball sideways. For a righty, positive numbers run arm-side.
